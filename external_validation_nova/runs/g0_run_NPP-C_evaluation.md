# G0 run `NPP-C` — evaluation

External worker, honour-based firewall, revision `e736800d`. Four deliverables returned in one pass
with no questions posed — which is compliance, not suspicion: the commission forbids asking the
commissioning party to decide anything.

## Integrity

**The standard was not patched.** Every one of the 32 documents in the sandbox is byte-identical
to the pinned commit. Trial 1's failure — a worker amending its own copy and then reading its own
text — did not repeat.

The findings register is not empty: nine findings across classes 3–6. That was the outcome to be
most suspicious of, and it did not occur.

## Finding F — answered, and against the nine

The author closed a vocabulary of **four kinds**: `platform-root`, `snapshot`, `inspection-surface`,
`evidence-record`.

Not nine, and not the same decomposition. There is no capability / workflow / event / intent split
anywhere in it. The four are sized to the profile's scope, which is what `2d` says a vocabulary is
for — a closed selection over an open language, owned by a profile.

**What this establishes:** the family does not lead an independent author to the reference
realization's set. Working from the standard alone, one was led somewhere else entirely.

**What it does not establish:** that the nine are wrong. This profile's scope is far narrower — one
system, one tenant, no external boundary — and a narrower scope legitimately admits fewer kinds. The
claim F actually tested was that the spaces are *canonical* and belong in the ontology. A canonical
set recognized by the standard would have pulled an independent author toward it. It pulled nowhere
near.

## Finding A — not answered, and this run could not answer it

**The instrument masked the finding it was meant to test.**

A records that `6a` gives an author the list of what to decide and no way to tell a deliberate
silence from an omission. The author distinguished them cleanly — class 3 against class 5, with
reasons. But that distinction came from **the commission**, which now hands over a six-class
taxonomy naming exactly that difference.

A clean log is therefore evidence that the commission works. It says nothing about whether `6a`
does. The taxonomy was added to make findings legible, and it supplies from outside the family
precisely what the finding says the family lacks.

## The ledger is inflated, and the taxonomy is why

**Seven of twelve class 2 entries cite "task commission" as their authority** — profile identity,
what it profiles, inspection required, no external boundary, no extra attestation, no replication,
one tenant.

Those are not decisions the family delegated. They are the scope the commission handed down in its
own §2. The commission's §4 says so itself: *"A ruling from the commissioning party is not the
standard."* Recorded as class 2 — *the standard delegated it to you and you decided it* — they
read as evidence the family is independently usable, and they are not evidence of that at all.

The author is not at fault. **The taxonomy has no class for "the commissioner decided this."**
Six classes cover realization freedom, profile-specified, underspecified, ambiguity, omission, and
reference-shaped assumption. Commissioner-supplied scope falls through into class 2.

This is the same defect as the Finding A masking, in a second place: the commission is doing work it
is supposed to be measuring.

## Findings worth carrying, pending verification

Two class 5 findings are checkable against the family and would matter if they hold:

- **No normative syntax, format, or schema for a profile artifact.** The family requires a profile
  identity and claims but supplies no form for the artifact carrying them, so a profile claim cannot
  be mechanically checked.
- **No claim vocabulary or registry.** Profiles support claims; nothing makes claim names
  interoperable between them.

Two class 6 findings concern trust-root structure and the authority mechanism for profile change.
Both are plausible and neither has been checked against `3e` and `2e`.

## Disposition

The run stands. Its integrity held and it produced a genuine result on **F**.

**Before the next run, the instrument needs two repairs**, both in the commission rather than the
standard:

1. A class for commissioner-supplied scope, so those decisions stop being counted as evidence the
   family delegates well.
2. Either withhold the taxonomy and require the author to draw the silence-versus-omission line
   unaided — which is what **A** actually asks — or accept that **A** cannot be tested by an
   instrument that answers it in advance.

Neither repair invalidates `NPP-C`. The profile and the F result stand on their own.
