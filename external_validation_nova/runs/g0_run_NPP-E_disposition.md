## The author was right about the facts and wrong about the register

Every entry describes something real, and the citations hold. Several of the author's own reasonings
say so outright — *"the family **expressly** supplies no trust root"*, *"the family **expressly**
leaves this residue"*. It knew these silences were declared.

**So the misplacement is not a failure to distinguish.** It is the mirror of run `NPP-C`'s: that
run filed commissioner scope as though the family had delegated it; this run filed family
delegations as though they were unresolved. Both directions inflate.

## The cause is a defect in the commission, and it is mine

The commission asks for a register of **"matters left unresolved"** and does not say *unresolved by
whom*. The author read it as *unresolved by the family* — which is exactly what a delegated decision
is, since delegation means the family declines to determine the value. On that reading the placement
was correct.

What was meant is *matters you could not resolve*. A decision the family delegated and the author
made is a **determination**, and belongs in the first register. That the register is titled
`findings_register.md` compounds it: a determination filed there reads as a finding against the
standard when it is the profile working.

**Repair for the next run:** name the registers by who is left holding the question, not by where
the silence originated.

## What this establishes about the standard

**Every point this author reached for was one the family had already marked.** `6a` §7 names three
of the eight; `4c` §8, `2d` §1 and `2b` §10 declare three more as outside their scope or openly
unresolved; `7b` §6 assigns fixtures to the claim.

That is the *does-not-specify* apparatus working. An author probing for the edges of the standard
found the edges the standard had already drawn, and found no undeclared ones.

**It is also a second, independent line of support for Finding A.** The author could tell a
declared silence from a gap — its reasoning marks them *expressly* — and produced no entry claiming
the family had failed to decide something it had not already said it would leave open.

## Disposition

- **F-01 … F-08: declined.** None is carried against `draft-4`. No repair to the standard follows
  from any of them.
- **One repair to the commission**, recorded above: rename the registers so *unresolved* means
  unresolved by the author.
- **Run `NPP-E` produced no findings against the standard.** For a probe of this kind that is a
  result, not an empty outcome.
